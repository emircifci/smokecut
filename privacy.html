Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SmokeCut ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create a profile, set your quit date, log your daily cigarette usage, or record crisis moments. The App uses Apple's native technologies (SwiftData/CoreData) to store this personal data locally on your device.

We may also use third-party analytics and crash reporting tools (such as Firebase Analytics) to better understand how the App is used. This data is generally anonymized and aggregated.

2. Data Storage and Security

Your highly sensitive personal data (such as health-related check-ins, slip records, and triggers) remains on your local device and your private Apple iCloud account (if synchronized). We do not own, sell, or rent your personal health data to marketers or third parties.

3. Local and Remote Configurations

We use Firebase Remote Config to push global settings (such as social proof numbers or feature flags) to your device. This process does not collect personally identifiable information but may collect basic diagnostic device metadata to ensure the update reaches your device properly.

4. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Our App offers premium features through Apple's App Store. When you make a purchase, the payment is processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your credit card information.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
